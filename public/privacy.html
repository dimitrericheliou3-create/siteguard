SiteGuard Privacy Policy

This is a draft policy and must be reviewed for the jurisdictions in which SiteGuard operates before launch.

Information we process

Account email addresses, password hashes, session tokens, monitored website URLs, monitoring results and subscription identifiers may be processed to provide the service.

Payments

Payments are handled by PayPal. SiteGuard does not store full payment card details.

Retention and requests

Monitoring history is retained for operational purposes. Account deletion, export and formal privacy request workflows must be implemented before public launch.
