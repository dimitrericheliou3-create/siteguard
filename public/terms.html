Service description

SiteGuard periodically requests public websites and records availability results. Monitoring can be affected by network conditions, firewalls, DNS, and third-party infrastructure. The service does not guarantee that a website will always be reachable or detect every incident.

Subscriptions

Paid plans are billed through PayPal. Pricing and plan limits are shown on the website. Cancellation and refund terms must be finalized before launch.

Acceptable use

Only monitor websites you own or are authorized to monitor. Do not use SiteGuard to probe private networks or systems without permission.
